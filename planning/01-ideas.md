# Ideas

This is a parking lot. Add anything that sounds fun. Do not cross ideas out for being too big, too small, or unfinished.

When you are ready to choose, mark one idea (or a combination) as **Chosen** and carry it into [02-from-idea-to-story.md](02-from-idea-to-story.md).
