# From idea to story

Chosen from [01-ideas.md](01-ideas.md). The first draft is in [../story/the-shark-nobody-would-sit-by.md](../story/the-shark-nobody-would-sit-by.md).

## The idea we chose

Working title: The shark nobody would sit by

The idea in one sentence: In an elementary school under the sea, the fish will not talk to Gerald the hammerhead, until Jeffrey the goldfish does.

Feeling path: The reader is irritated at the fish for not talking to the shark, then sad when Gerald goes home alone, then happy when he and Jeffrey become friends.

## The person at the center

- Who are they? Gerald, a hammerhead shark. His friend is Jeffrey, the only goldfish in the class. Jeremiah and Donald are clownfish in the same class. Their teacher is an ocean sunfish named Teacher.
- What do they want? Gerald wants a friend.
- Why does it matter to them? No one wants to be his friend, and he goes home sad. Jeffrey can relate, because he is the only goldfish in the classroom.
- What is in the way? The other fish think Gerald is going to eat them, so no one will sit by him or talk to him.
- What happens if they fail? Gerald stays alone.
- How are they different at the end? Gerald and Jeffrey leave school behind, swim into the ocean, and become the bestest of friends.

## The world of the story

- Where and when does it happen? An elementary school. A classroom, then Gerald's home, then school again the next morning, then out in the ocean after they leave school behind.
- What is one rule of this world that matters to the story? Fish and a shark go to elementary school together, and the fish believe a shark will eat them.
- What should the reader be able to picture? A classroom with Gerald sitting apart, because nobody will sit next to the hammerhead. Jeffrey is the only goldfish. Jeremiah and Donald are clownfish. Teacher, an ocean sunfish, is the teacher.

## The shape

1. Beginning — The class will not talk to Gerald. The reader is irritated at the fish for staying away from him. The fish think he is going to eat them all.
2. Middle — Gerald goes home sad because no one wanted to be his friend. The next morning, like always, no one wants to sit by him.
3. End — Jeffrey swims up and starts to talk, because he knows what it is like to be the only one of his kind. They talk about being friends, how big Gerald is, and how small Jeffrey is. They leave school behind, swim into the ocean, and become the bestest of friends.
