## The person at the center

- Who are they?
- What do they want?
- Why does it matter to them?
- What is in the way?
- What happens if they fail?
- How are they different at the end?

## The world of the story

- Where and when does it happen?
- What is one rule of this world that matters to the story?
- What should the reader be able to picture?

## The shape

Tell the story in three beats first. Details come after this feels right.

1. Beginning — the ordinary moment, and the moment it changes:
2. Middle — the hardest trouble, and what the main person tries:
3. End — how it resolves, and what it cost or changed:
